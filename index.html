Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-20

What Bloom keeps

Your name, date, time and region of birth, your settings, and which scenes and verses you have met. Bloom uses them only to shape your sessions.

Where it is kept

Only on this device, in the app's local storage. Bloom has no account and no server. Nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else.

What reaches the internet

Nothing. Fonts, sounds and the calendar all ship inside the app. Bloom has no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Subscriptions

Bloom+ is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; Bloom never sees your payment details or Apple ID. The app only asks the App Store, on this device, whether your subscription is active.

How to erase it

Settings → Erase my data removes everything Bloom stored on this device. Deleting the app does the same.

Children

Bloom is not meant for children under 13.
